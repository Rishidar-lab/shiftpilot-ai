## What the submission must NOT claim

- **No claim that Claude is verified.** The Claude adapter exists but has never made a
  live call; the verified provider is OpenRouter on the free tier.
- **No accuracy percentages.** The 16/16 is a controlled corpus outcome, not a benchmark.
- **No fixed underlying model on `openrouter/free`** — the alias rotates across the free
  pool; the eval used an explicitly configured `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it:free`.
- **No monetary guarantees.** Spend controls are a brake, not a cap; there is no
  "production-ready" claim and no authentication — all deliberate, documented Week-1
  scopes (`README.md` "Current limitations").
